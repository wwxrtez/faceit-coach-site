Counter-Strike 2 · Performance insights

Understand your game.
Improve with intent.

FACEIT Coach is a desktop companion concept for reviewing public FACEIT CS2 statistics, comparing recent form with longer-term performance, and turning observations into a practical training plan.

Built for players who want clear, useful feedback

FACEIT Coach is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by FACEIT or Valve.

01 / PROFILE

One search field

Search by FACEIT nickname or profile link. Steam links can be used when the linked FACEIT account can be identified.

02 / TRENDS

Recent form and history

Compare available CS2 statistics across recent-match windows and lifetime summaries, without inventing missing data.

03 / PRACTICE

Actionable next steps

Use performance patterns to guide a simple weekly practice plan and track progress over time.

Current status

FACEIT Coach is under active development. Features that depend on official FACEIT data access require the developer's approved integration and may not be available in every prototype build.

Contact and privacy

Read the Privacy Notice to understand how the desktop prototype handles profile queries and locally saved application data. For project support, use the contact method provided by the developer who distributed your build.
