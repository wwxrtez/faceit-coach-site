Built around useful signals

From raw numbers to a clearer picture.

The goal is not another noisy dashboard. It is a calm place to understand what changed and what may be worth working on.

One profile search

Start with a FACEIT nickname or profile link. Steam links can be supported when the account relationship can be identified.

Recent form vs. history

Compare available match windows with longer-term summaries. Missing data should stay missing—not be replaced with guesses.

Practical next steps

Use performance patterns as input for a realistic practice plan, without overloading the player with unnecessary metrics.
